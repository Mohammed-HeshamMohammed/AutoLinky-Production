# The search, reconsidered

The homepage opens with a 24-second, silent motion-graphics film. The story moves from repeated forms to a CV, matched jobs and applications at the user's chosen pace. Scrolling seeks the actual MP4 in either direction; translated HTML carries the narrative so it stays readable and accessible in English and Arabic. The imagery uses existing AutoLinky app screenshots, labeled as sample data.

`src/ScrollFilm.jsx` owns the timeline, playback, chapter controls, keyboard range and transcript. `src/brandFilm.js` owns the bilingual story. The section uses ordinary document scrolling and a sticky stage; it does not capture wheel/touch input. Skip film moves focus to the next section. Reduced motion and short landscape viewports remove the long pinned section and expose the transcript. Missing or unsupported media retains the poster, signup action and readable story, including errors that happen before hydration. Without JavaScript, the poster and transcript remain available without a long pinned section.

The movie is original programmatic motion graphics, rendered from `scripts/render-brand-film.mjs`; it is not AI-generated live-action footage. Runway was discovered but its account is not connected, so no Runway video was generated. No external footage, fabricated product screens or new icon pack is used. A sourced photorealistic iPhone hardware frame replaces the earlier drawn outline; actual safe-area app renders sit behind it. [Frame provenance, shared geometry and screen reproduction](DEVICE_MOCKUP.md). Film labels and app imagery are in English; the accompanying narrative and controls are localized.

The opening paperwork uses complete, typeset sample documents instead of schematic cards and placeholder bars: a CV, a cover letter and three different application forms repeat the same candidate's details. A generated blank paper material supplies subtle fibers and lighting; the documents are neither photographs nor scans of a real person's paperwork. `scripts/film-document-content.json` keeps their text editable, while `scripts/film-documents.mjs` renders five A4 WebP assets at 1000 × 1414. The source material lives at `scripts/assets/paper-sheet.png`; the renderer crops its flat interior to exclude the generated cutout fringe. All candidate details, employers and education are fictional, use a reserved example.com contact address and carry a small sample-document footer. [Document provenance, layout and reproduction](FILM_DOCUMENTS.md).

The shipped media is `public/assets/film/autolinky-story.mp4` (1080 × 1080, 24 fps, H.264, no audio) and its WebP poster. Fast-start metadata and a keyframe every half second keep seeking practical. Video loads metadata initially; there is no autoplay. The player URL includes the `mobile-editorial-2` media revision to invalidate earlier cached footage. `server.js` serves MP4/WebM MIME types, byte ranges, suffix/open ranges, HEAD and unsatisfiable-range responses so browser seeking also works on the Node production server.

To reproduce, run from `frontend/` with Node, an optional installation of `@napi-rs/canvas` (validated with 0.1.100) and FFmpeg (validated with 7.1):

```text
node scripts/render-brand-film.mjs --modules <absolute-node_modules-directory> --ffmpeg <absolute-ffmpeg-executable>
```

The renderer uses the bundled Manrope font, prepared safe-area images in `public/assets/app/iphone/`, and the raster hardware/aperture from `src/iphoneMockup.json`. Its document helper combines the editable content and blank material into `public/assets/film/documents/*.webp` before rendering the film. The detail callout uses the refreshed unframed matches image, cropped to the current job card. Optional render tools stay outside production dependencies. It writes the movie/poster to public assets and inspection stills into ignored `output/playwright/film-stills/`. Editing the translated HTML narrative does not require rerendering the video. Editing document text, material or layout requires rebuilding its images and the film. The existing iPhone scenes, 24-second duration and 24 fps timeline remain unchanged.

The branding update refreshes the four product images from actual Flutter widget renders with deterministic sample data: `06_matches_en`, `07_profile_en`, `43_auto_apply_preferences_en` and `24_explore_en` in the shared app's screenshot suite. They are converted to WebP, and the film is rerendered with the current mobile palette. These fixtures do not represent live accounts, jobs or outcomes.

Validation and release state are recorded in `WORKSPACE_DESIGN.md` and the repository `PLAN.md`. Local verification does not mean the website has been deployed.
