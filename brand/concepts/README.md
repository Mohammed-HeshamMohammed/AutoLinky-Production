# Logo exploration

The owner rejected an abstract two-link mark because it did not reference AutoLinky, then rejected a literal AL monogram as insufficiently creative. The requested complete identity includes light/dark, text, short, minimal and monochrome versions.

The built-in image-generation tool produced `auto-link-directions.png`. The owner selected **01 — A with linked crossbar**. Its A references the name and its crossbar becomes a connected return path. The production mark was reconstructed in `../identity.json` using editable filled vector paths. All exported variants derive from that geometry; the concept sheet is a reference, not a runtime asset.

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen.imagegen`, not CLI. Prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: professional identity exploration board for AutoLinky, a job-matching and application-automation product. Create a premium landscape 2-by-2 logo concept board on warm white #FBFBF9. Four genuinely different original, exceptionally well-designed vector-style symbols, one large symbol per quadrant, clearly separated by generous whitespace; tiny labels 01, 02, 03, 04 only. All four should combine a recognizable capital A with the idea of an automatic connected route or joining two things. Do NOT just typeset AL or draw A next to L. Each symbol must be one cohesive, ownable, clever silhouette, simple enough for a 24-pixel app icon. Explore: (01) an A made from a single broad continuous path whose lower right stroke loops back through the crossbar like an actual link; (02) two precise interlocking ribbon pieces that create the A in negative space, conveying matching two sides; (03) an architectural A joined to a small open-loop return path, suggesting automation, with a subtle forward direction in the negative space; (04) a bold paperclip-like connected A, with a beautiful distinctive cut and one nested join. Flat fills or constant-width paths, refined negative space, optical balance, skilled brand designer execution. Restrained palette: charcoal #171C19 as main mark color, muted olive #5F7253 as a supporting portion of each symbol only. No wordmark, no extra letters, no generic chain/infinity symbol, no gradients, no glows, no 3D, no mockups, no stock symbols, no robots, no circuit boards, no briefcases, no sparks, no decorative background. This is a serious creative identity exploration, not a generic monogram sheet.

## Lettering refinement

The owner then requested that the mark serve as the first A in the name, without repeating an A beside it, and asked for matching typography. The primary logo now integrates the selected bare linked A with outlined Sora 670 lettering: [linked A]utoLinky. The selected symbol geometry is retained; the square is reserved for compact icons. Sora source/license are in ../fonts/Sora/.

The subsequent owner refinement added a smaller returning link to the L's foot in the integrated full logo. It retains a clear vertical L stem and uses the A's olive/sage role. A later exploration also customized t and y with small returning terminals. **That t/y exploration is superseded:** the owner restored the ordinary Sora t and y while keeping the selected linked A and custom L.

The current full wordmark is 405 × 80. Only A and L use custom geometry in `../identity.json`; the other letters, including t and y, use the unmodified Sora 670 outlines. The A remains the lead and sole standalone icon. Formation draws the A's link and L's return from their visible attachment points toward their free tips, while the ordinary letters reveal without extra hooks. SVG/PNG, monochrome and animated exports all follow this current composition.
